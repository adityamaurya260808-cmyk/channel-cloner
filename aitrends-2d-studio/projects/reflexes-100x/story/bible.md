# Bible: "What If Your Reflexes Were 100x Faster?"
**Logline:** second person. "You" are Theo, 14, whose brain reacts 100x faster than normal while his body stays ordinary. Wonder and comedy turn into loneliness, then a sports-day climax where being *on time* beats being fast.
**Tone:** wonder → playful → melancholy → suspense → warm triumph → mystery → explainer.
