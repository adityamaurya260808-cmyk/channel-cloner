# Psychology of Focus: shot list

213 shots across 69 narration beats, about 15.2 shots per minute at ~14 min (each shot ~3-4 s).

Generate each shot as an image (or ~4 s video clip) in Flow. Shots of one beat play back-to-back over that beat's narration. Add a slow zoom or pan in the editor for motion.

**Character:** Pip (round head, dot eyes, orange hoodie). **Style prefix** (already in every prompt): 2D flat vector cartoon explainer style, thick clean outlines, muted pastel palette with warm orange accents, simple minimal background, 16:9. Main character Pip: round head, two dot eyes, small mouth, orange hoodie, no nose.
