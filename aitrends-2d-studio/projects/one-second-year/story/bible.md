# Bible: "What If One Second Felt Like a Year?"
**Logline:** second person. "You" are Leo, 15. Hold your breath and one second lasts a whole year in your mind, but your body (even your eyes) stays frozen until you breathe out. The power wins maths tests, chess and penalties, but costs forty lonely years. Grandpa gives him a song for the silence; when Grandpa collapses, Leo spends a frozen year rehearsing CPR, then uses the real seconds, pushing to the beat of Grandpa's disco song.
**Tone:** warm comedy → wonder → melancholy → suspense → tearful triumph → mystery → explainer.
