# What If You Invested $1 Every Day for 40 Years? — story bible

**Logline:** A glass jar, one dollar a day and 33 untouched years. Then a 3 a.m. scam call forces "you" (Sam) to choose between the number and Mom.
**Tone:** Warm drama with a thriller cold open and a "Part Two" sting. Second person: "you" = Sam.
**Voice:** Kokoro `am_michael` (local neural TTS), one narrator, script word-for-word. 10:22.
